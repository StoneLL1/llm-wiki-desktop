# Purpose

> Personal growth knowledge base. Capture principles, habits, and lessons as a linked journal of self-improvement.

## Goal

Turn reflections, articles, and course notes into an actionable map of how I want to grow.

## Key Questions

- What principles do I want to live by?
- Which habits and practices are working?
- What have I learned from setbacks?

## Scope

Mix your own reflections with imported articles. Keep entries honest, dated, and linked to related ideas.

## Direction

Use `wiki/concepts/` for principles and frameworks, `wiki/sources/` for books and articles you learn from, and `wiki/queries/` for honest self-questions.
