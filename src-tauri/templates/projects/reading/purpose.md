# Purpose

> Reading notes knowledge base. Distill books, essays, and long-form reading into linked takeaways.

## Goal

Remember what I read. Turn books and articles into a connected web of insights instead of forgotten highlights.

## Key Questions

- What is this book really about?
- Which ideas changed how I think?
- How does this connect to other things I have read?

## Scope

One page per book or major work. Pull out concepts and quotes; link recurring themes across books.

## Direction

Use `wiki/entities/` for authors, `wiki/sources/` for each book, and `wiki/synthesis/` for cross-book themes.
