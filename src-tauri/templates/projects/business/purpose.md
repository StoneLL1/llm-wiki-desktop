# Purpose

> Business knowledge base. Organize market intel, competitors, customers, and internal decisions into one linked reference.

## Goal

Give a team or founder a single source of truth for the people, companies, and decisions that shape the business.

## Key Questions

- Who are our customers, competitors, and partners?
- What decisions have we made and why?
- Where does our information come from?

## Scope

Combine internal notes with external articles and reports. Keep every claim linkable to its source.

## Direction

Use `wiki/entities/` for companies and people, `wiki/synthesis/` for market themes, and `wiki/comparisons/` for competitive analysis.
