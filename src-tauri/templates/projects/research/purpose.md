# Purpose

> Research knowledge base. Track sources, claims, and evidence for an ongoing investigation.

## Goal

Build a citable evidence base from papers, articles, datasets, and notes so that every claim traces back to a source.

## Key Questions

- What is the central thesis I am testing?
- Which sources support or contradict each claim?
- Where is the evidence strongest and where are the gaps?

## Scope

Favor primary sources and explicit citations. Capture conflicting views as their own pages rather than merging them away.

## Direction

Use `wiki/sources/` for each reference, `wiki/synthesis/` for cross-source findings, and `wiki/comparisons/` for competing positions.
